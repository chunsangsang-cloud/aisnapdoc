Subscription

The first 10 pages of automatic recognition are free. SnapDoc Pro (yearly) unlocks unlimited recognition and Excel export. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, open Settings in the app → SnapDoc Pro → Restore purchases.

Your data

All data stays on your iPhone. We cannot see or recover it. You can delete everything in the app under Settings → Delete all data.
